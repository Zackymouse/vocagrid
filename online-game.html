VOCAGRID

How to Play

  • Players take turns selecting tiles to make an English word (2 letters or more).
  • When you submit a valid word, those tiles become your color and stay on the board.
  • If you surround one of your own tiles on all four sides, it becomes locked and cannot be captured.
  • You gain +1 point for each tile you convert to your color.
  • The game ends when all tiles are taken or when both players pass consecutively.

Word Score & Effects

  • 2–3 letters: +1 bonus
  • 4–5 letters: +3 bonus
  • 6–7 letters: +5 bonus
  • 8+ letters: +8 bonus + special effects (coming soon!)

Controls

  • Tap tiles to select; tap again to deselect.
  • Tap & hold a selected tile to reorder your word.
  • Use Undo to remove the last selected tile.
  • Use Clear to reset your selection.
