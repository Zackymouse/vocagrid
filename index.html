VOCAGRID

How to Play

  • Players take turns selecting tiles to make an English word (2 letters or more).
  • When you submit a valid word, those tiles become your color and stay on the board.
  • If you surround one of your own tiles on all four sides with your tiles, that tile becomes locked. Locked tiles cannot be used by your opponent, but you can still use them. If the surrounding tiles are taken back, the lock is broken.
  • You gain +1 point for each tile that was neutral and becomes yours for the first time.
  • The game ends when all 25 tiles are owned. The player with the higher total points wins.
